What it is

A break you can close in one click

Ludus sits next to the clock. It opens as a small panel over whatever you are doing and closes the moment you click elsewhere. No windows to manage, no accounts, no ads.

Picks up where you left off

Close the panel mid-game and it pauses. Open it later and the game is still there, along with your best scores.

Rounds that fit a short break

Every game is built to be played in a minute or two, with keyboard controls and mouse or trackpad where it makes sense.

Private and offline

Everything runs on your computer. Ludus never connects to the internet and collects nothing.

Install

Two minutes, then it lives by the clock

Ludus is not sold through an app store, so your computer asks you to confirm the first time you open it. That is expected; the steps below show where to click.

Mac

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon (M1 or newer)

  1. Download Ludus-mac.zip and open it. You get Ludus.app.
  2. Drag Ludus.app into Applications.
  3. The first time only: in Applications, right-click Ludus and choose Open, then Open again. On newer macOS versions, if you see no Open button, go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
  4. Look for the controller icon in the menu bar and click it.

Why the extra step: this build is not notarised by Apple yet, so macOS cannot check the developer for you.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

  1. Download Ludus-windows.zip, right-click it and choose Extract All. Keep the Ludus folder somewhere permanent, for example Documents.
  2. Open the folder and run Ludus.exe.
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. If Windows asks for the .NET Desktop Runtime (or WebView2 on some Windows 10 PCs), accept the free Microsoft download, then run Ludus.exe again.
  5. Click the controller icon in the system tray. If you cannot see it, click the small arrow next to the tray icons.

Why the warning: this build is not code signed yet, so SmartScreen does not recognise it.

Questions

Good to know

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every game and every sound is inside the app. Ludus never needs, or uses, an internet connection.

Where are my scores and saved games kept?

On your own computer only. On a Mac they are kept in the app's preferences; on Windows in a file at %APPDATA%\Ludus\state.json. Closing the panel or quitting keeps your place.

What does Ludus collect about me?

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind, and nothing you do in Ludus leaves your computer.

How do I open it when my computer starts?

Turn on "Open at login" in Ludus settings (the gear icon), or on Windows right-click the tray icon.

What keys do the games use?

Arrow keys or WASD for most games, Space to act, Esc or P to pause. Each game shows its own controls under the play area. Two-player games share one keyboard.

How do I remove it?

Turn off "Open at login" and quit Ludus from its settings. On a Mac, drag Ludus from Applications to the Bin. On Windows, delete the Ludus folder, and %APPDATA%\Ludus if you also want to remove your scores.

Is it open source?

Yes, under the MIT licence. The code, and instructions for building it yourself, are on GitHub.
